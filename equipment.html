Materials, by what they're for

Picking a material is picking a job, not a color

PLA / PETG

General-purpose. Fast, cheap, plenty strong for interior trim, brackets away from heat, and anything cosmetic.

ABS / ASA

Better heat and UV resistance than PLA — the pick for anything that lives outside or near warm engine-bay air.

TPU

Flexible and abrasion-resistant. Gaskets, bushings, anything that needs to flex or absorb an impact instead of cracking.

Composite (CF / GF)

Carbon- and glass-filled filaments bring added stiffness, a clean matte finish, and a few extra CF / GF swag points. Great when the part needs to feel solid and look the part, too.

PPA-CF

The high-temperature option, for parts mounted closest to the engine or exhaust where ABS would eventually give up.
