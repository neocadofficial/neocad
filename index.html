How the shop works

Three things worth knowing before you order

Fitment

Reverse-engineered, not eyeballed

Tricky fits get scanned with a blue-laser 3D scanner before anything is modeled, so the part matches the factory geometry instead of an approximation.

See the scanner
Fleet

Built for engine-bay conditions

Composite and high-temp filaments run on enclosed, heated-chamber printers, so parts near heat and vibration are printed in materials that can actually take it.

See the machines
Pricing

The math is shown, not hidden

Every quote is material cost plus machine time plus labor, the same formula every time. Plug in your own numbers and watch the price build.
