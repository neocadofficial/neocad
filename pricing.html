Pricing guide

How a quote actually gets built

Every job is priced the same way: material cost, plus machine time, plus any hands-on labor, plus a small markup and contingency. No flat "per part" guessing. Here's the actual rate card, and a tool at the bottom to try it yourself.

The fine print on every quote

5% material markup on top of raw filament cost.

5% production contingency on materials + machine time, to cover failed prints and re-runs.

$0.25/hour for shared dryer time on moisture-sensitive filaments.

$10 minimum order before shipping and tax — nothing ships for less.
