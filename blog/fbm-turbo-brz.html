My 2013 FBM Turbo BRZ: daily duties, drift plans

September 22, 2026·BRZ / Street & drift

If you're local, you probably know this as the pink BRZ. It's black again now. I recently pulled the wrap off, so the locally iconic pink era is taking a break while the car gets some black downtime before the next color. Same car, slightly harder to spot in a parking lot.

Half street, half drift

This is my personal daily, and I'm getting it ready to split its time between normal street use and drifting. Recent mods include GKTech solid diff risers, my own transmission mount bushings, a GKTech V3 bolt-on angle kit, and adjustable arms. Basically getting the rest of the car ready for the things I keep asking it to do.

Underneath all that, it's still a stock FA20 with around 65k miles, boosted for about half its life. The FBM setup runs a GTX2867R, flex fuel, and electronic boost control. I also put a hood exit on it because I wanted to see flames. Monkey brain won that discussion.

Things I can just make now

A lot of the printing starts with something I want for this car, but it isn't always my idea. The quick-release steering wheel hanger came from a customer request: somewhere to hang the wheel up and out of the way using a stock-clip replacement design. That's exactly the kind of stuff I want to keep making.

I've also printed OFT tablet holders, FR-S fog-light brake ducts, decorative pieces, and other little bits. There are plenty more ideas for my own car, and I want to make more cool stuff for the rest of the community too. If you've got an annoying little problem that needs a part, that's a pretty good place to start.

Eventually, FA24 :)

All of this is prep for the eventual FA24 swap. For now, the FA20 is still doing daily duty while I work through the rest of the car. One thing at a time, allegedly.

I also have a built 1993 Mitsubishi 3000GT VR-4, but that's a whole separate post. This one has enough going on already.
