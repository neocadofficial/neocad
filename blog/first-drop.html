First product drop: the bushing set is live

Shop update · BRZ / FR-S / 86

This one started small: a trans mount with too much play in it, a bit of leftover filament, and a rough idea for something to fill the gap. It wasn't supposed to be a product — it was supposed to fix one car.

The first version, up above, was really just a shape test — does something like this even fit in the mount without interfering with the factory rubber or the bolts? It did. From there it was a matter of dialing in the geometry against the actual mount instead of guessing at it.

Why TPU instead of something rigid

The instinct with a part like this is to reach for something stiff — a carbon-fiber-reinforced filament, printed hard as a rock. That's the wrong material for this job. The stock mount already has rubber doing the damping; a bushing set that's too rigid just moves the slop somewhere else instead of controlling it. TPU 95A is firm enough to cut the play down, but it still flexes and absorbs impact the way the mount is designed to work in the first place.

The production version is a cleaner two-piece design, printed in black instead of the prototype purple, and fits back in using the same mount and hardware the car already has. No cutting, no drilling, nothing permanent.

It's been through enough of these one at a time that it stopped making sense to keep treating it as a favor. So it's a real listing now: $40 shipped, $35 for local pickup. Etsy and Shopify are next, but until those are live, ordering is as simple as sending a message.

More of these write-ups are coming as new parts get designed — some will be car stuff, some won't be. If it's worth printing, it's probably worth writing down.
