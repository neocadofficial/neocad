First product drop: the bushing set is live

Shop update · BRZ / FR-S / 86

This one started with a simple premise. Remove drivetrain slop and improve driver experience, without paying for overpriced options elsewhere.

The first version, up above, was really just a shape test — does something like this even fit in the mount without interfering with the factory rubber or the bolts? After a very close first try, the second test print turned out with great tolerances. Now to pick a material.

Why TPU instead of something stiff?

The instinct with a part like this is to reach for something stiff — a carbon-fiber-reinforced filament, printed hard as a rock. That's the wrong material for this job. The stock mount already has rubber doing the damping; a bushing set that's too rigid just moves the slop somewhere else instead of controlling it. TPU 95A is firm enough to cut the play down, but it still flexes and absorbs impact the way the mount is designed to work in the first place. If it bends and flexes just enough, it can bounce back instead of cracking and snapping.

The production version is a cleaner two-piece design, printed in black instead of the prototype purple, and fits back in using the same mount and hardware the car already has. No cutting, no drilling, nothing permanent. Super easy install. Firmer TPU coming soon for you race-car drivers, along with a bright new color for the next batch!

With the local interest I got, I decided to actually get the word out to my online fellow 86ers to be able to experience that sweet sweet gear whine: $40 shipped, $35 for local pickup. Etsy and Shopify are next, but until those are live, ordering is as simple as sending a message.

More of these write-ups are coming as new parts get designed — some will be car stuff, some won't be. If it's worth printing, it's probably worth writing down.
