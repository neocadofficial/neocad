Product details

Choose your feel

Black or pink, full-walled 95A TPU: firmer driver feel with some flex.

Blue 72D race spec: much stiffer, for the drifty track boys.

Half walls / half infill: a less rigid option that still fills the gaps and improves mount feel. $25.00 pickup or $32.50 shipped. Ask about available colors when ordering.

The inserts take up gaps in the factory mount, reduce excess movement and bring out gear whine in 1st, 2nd and some of 3rd. Added noise and vibration vary with your setup.

Fitment

First-generation BRZ / FR-S / Toyota 86 and second-generation BRZ / Toyota GR86, manual transmission. Both generations use the same transmission mount.

Installation · about 30 minutes

With the vehicle safely supported, drop the front pipe, support the transmission with a jack, and remove the mount. Fit the inserts, then reinstall the mount and front pipe using the factory procedure and torque specifications. Factory hardware is reused.
