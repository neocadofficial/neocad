Transmission Mount Bushing Set

Subaru BRZ Scion FR-S Toyota 86

The factory rubber transmission mount has enough give in it to let the whole drivetrain shift under hard launches, clutch drops, and aggressive shifting. This bushing set sits inside the stock mount and takes up that slack — same mounting points, same bolts, nothing cut or drilled.

Designed to fill the gaps in the factory mount, these inserts reduce excess movement and give the driver a more connected feel. They also bring out a nice gear whine in 1st, 2nd, and some of 3rd — a little more transmission character in the cabin. Expect some added noise and vibration; how much you hear depends on your setup.

Fitment
2013–2024 BRZ / FR-S / 86 (ZC6 / ZN6), manual transmission
Material

Installation overview

Allow about 30 minutes with access underneath the car. With the vehicle safely supported, drop the front pipe for clearance, keep a jack supporting the transmission, and remove the transmission mount. Fit the inserts, then reinstall the mount and front pipe using the factory service procedure and torque specifications.
